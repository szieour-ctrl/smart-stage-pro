Photo being staged — directions like "left wall" or "foreground" refer to this view.
IF the staging engine didn’t list or find an anchor to place the zone below, you can add an anchor or placement direction to any zone or room. You should identify placement for the less obvious zone.
Examples:
Dining Zone: Anchor: Open space foreground right
Dining Zone: Anchor: Open space in the background left near “___”
Living Room: Anchor: Wall on the left
Living Room: Anchor: Wall on the left with 2 windows
Areas: left · right · background · foreground left / center / right
Room Read — Edit Before Approving
✏️ Edit the room read directly before staging. This is what the Smart Stage engine detected in your photo — which zones it found and where it anchored them. Add instructions, fix a misread zone, or remove a placement detail; whatever you leave here is used exactly as written. Your style, palette, and compliance settings from earlier are applied automatically and aren't shown here.
⚠ Open Plan detected. The Smart Stage engine read this as a multi-zone space. Watch for zone crowding — if the prompt places dining furniture AND living furniture in the same area, delete the placement instructions and keep only style/material descriptions. The engine's native open-plan logic handles zone separation better than text instructions.
